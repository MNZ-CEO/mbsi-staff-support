Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how MBSI Staff (operated by BOLA TV, MCHJ for MBSI school) handles your information. The app is intended only for school employees whose accounts are created by the school administration.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, username, phone number, role and branch, provided by the school administration.
  • Work records — attendance (check-in and check-out times), tasks, salary, bonuses and deductions.
  • Location — your location is checked only at the moment you check in or out, to confirm you are on the school premises. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Photos and files — only the photos or files you choose to attach (for example, to a task or a request).
  • Device information — device identifier, device model and a push notification token, used to keep your session secure and to deliver notifications.
  • Crash reports — technical crash data (via Firebase Crashlytics) to fix errors. It does not include your work records.

Face ID / fingerprint unlock is handled entirely by your phone. We never receive your biometric data.

How we use information

Only to provide the app's functions: attendance, payroll, tasks, notifications and school management. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking across other apps or websites.

Sharing

Your data is visible only to authorized staff of your school according to their role. We use service providers to run the app: cloud hosting and storage (DigitalOcean) and Google Firebase (notifications and crash reports). They process data only on our behalf.

Storage and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on secured servers. Access requires a personal login.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while you are employed by the school and as required by law. To request deletion of your account and personal data, contact us below.

Children

MBSI Staff is for adult school employees and is not intended for children.
