Frequently asked questions

How do I get an account?
Accounts are created by the school administration. The app has no public sign-up.

I forgot my password.
Ask your school administrator to reset it.

Check-in says I am not at school.
Allow location access for MBSI Staff in your phone settings and make sure you are on the school premises.

I do not receive notifications.
Allow notifications for MBSI Staff in your phone settings and sign in again.

How do I delete my account?
Contact the school administration or the support contacts above. Your account and personal data will be deleted, except records the school is required to keep by law.
